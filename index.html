About Me

Passionate about
open-source Android

I'm a developer from Shimla, India, specializing in Android custom ROM and kernel development. My work revolves around device bring-up, SELinux policy development, and building performant, stable Android experiences from source.

Beyond Android, I manage build infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, cloud servers, and contribute to web development through Nuvio Studio. I believe in the power of open source to make technology better for everyone.

When I'm not compiling ROMs or writing kernel patches, I'm exploring Linux systems, contributing to the community, and helping other developers get started in Android modding.
